About

I start with the business question, not the algorithm.

I'm drawn to data that has real stakes attached — where a wrong number costs someone money, or a missed pattern lets fraud slip through. That's what pulled me toward analytics in the first place.

During a data analytics internship, I replaced a manual weekly spreadsheet process with a self-serve Power BI dashboard for loan-default risk, after first cleaning and reconciling 10,000+ applicant records from multiple sources. Outside of that, I've built three full analytics projects — sales performance, customer churn, and credit card fraud detection — each one carried from raw data through EDA, modeling, and a dashboard someone could actually act on.

Day to day I work in SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn) and Power BI, and I care as much about whether a stakeholder can read the dashboard in under a minute as I do about the model behind it.
